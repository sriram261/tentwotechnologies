FAQ

Common Amazon store and selling questions

Quick answers for customers and product owners who want to understand the store, GST, Amazon fees, and product review support.

Does the calculator include GST?

Yes. It separates customer selling price before GST, output GST collected, purchase GST credit, GST on Amazon fees, and estimated GST payable.

Does it include Amazon fees?

Yes. It includes referral fee, closing fee, shipping/FBA fee, and 18% GST on Amazon fees. You should still verify the final fee inside Seller Central.

Can I submit my product to you?

Yes. You can submit your product details. We review eligible products and discuss possible Amazon India selling support based on product fit, pricing, quality, and margin.

Will you sell every product?

No. We review the product first. Selling support depends on product category, quality, pricing, packaging, compliance, margin, and Amazon marketplace fit.

Is profit guaranteed?

No. Product review reduces blind risk, but sales depend on demand, competition, reviews, pricing, ads, quality, and Amazon performance.

What product details should I send?

Send product category, photos if available, supplier cost, expected selling price, packaging details, quantity, and any competitor links.
