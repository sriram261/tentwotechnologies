Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you apply.

Is this a paid internship with a stipend?
No. This is a paid training program, not a stipend internship. You pay a one-time program fee of ₹3,500, and in return you get structured mentorship, real project work, reviews, and a verified certificate. We do not pay a stipend.
Who is this program for?
Students, final-year students, freshers, and early-career professionals who want practical, hands-on experience in a tech domain. If you want to build real projects and have something solid to show on your resume, this is for you.
Do I need prior experience?
No. Each track starts from the fundamentals and builds up through guided tasks. Basic familiarity with your chosen domain helps, but it is not required.
How long is the program and how much time does it take?
The program runs for 8 weeks. It is online and flexible, so you can work through the tasks at your own pace around college or a job. Plan for a few focused hours each week.
What does the ₹3,500 fee cover?
One-time fee per track. It covers 1-on-1 mentorship, learning resources, real project tasks, reviews and feedback, community access, and your completion certificate.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes. After you submit and complete your final project, you receive a verified completion certificate from TenTwo Technologies Private Limited.
Is the fee refundable?
The program fee is non-refundable. Once your spot is confirmed, your seat and resources are reserved for you. If you have any questions about the program, please email info@tentwotechnologies.com before applying.
When does the next batch start?
We onboard new participants regularly. Once you apply, we will email you to confirm your start date and next steps.
How do I apply?
Click Apply now, fill out the short form, and choose your preferred track. We will get back to you with the next steps.

Ready to apply?

Spots are limited each cohort. Submit your application and we'll get back to you with next steps.
