Common questions

Quick answers for businesses before requesting a quote.

What type of projects can TenTwo Technologies build?

We build business websites, landing pages, portfolios, dashboards, custom software, web apps, mobile apps, e-commerce stores, AI automation tools, APIs, and cloud setup.

How much does a project usually cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, number of pages, features, integrations, design work, and timeline. The prices shown on this page are starting points. After reviewing your requirement, we share a clear quote before starting work.

How long does it take to complete a website or software project?

A simple website can usually be completed faster, while custom software, dashboards, and mobile apps need more time based on features. Once you share the requirement, we provide a realistic timeline along with the quote.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We can work with clients in India, the United States, and other countries. You can share your country code and preferred contact method in the quote form.

Can you help with domain, hosting, and deployment?

Yes. We can guide you with domain setup, hosting, deployment, basic SEO, contact forms, analytics, backups, and cloud deployment depending on the project.

Can you update or fix an existing website?

Yes. We can help with text changes, image updates, new sections, contact form fixes, mobile responsiveness, performance improvements, and small bug fixes.

Do you provide support after the project is completed?

Yes. We can provide maintenance and support plans for updates, bug fixes, backups, security checks, and future improvements.

What happens after I submit the quote form?

We review your project details and contact you through your selected contact method. Usually, we reply within 24 hours with the next steps, questions if needed, and a clear quote plan.
