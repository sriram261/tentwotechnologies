How Much Does a Website Cost in India in 2026?

Ask five people and you will hear five prices, from ₹3,000 to ₹3,00,000. Here is what businesses in India actually pay in 2026, and what the money buys at each level, so you can judge any quote with clear eyes.

The three routes, priced in rupees

RouteTypical cost in 2026What you get
DIY builder (Wix, WordPress.com)₹8,000 to ₹20,000 a yearTemplates and your own weekends. The yearly fees add up, and the site rarely looks like a real business made it.
Freelancer₹10,000 to ₹40,000 one timeA working site at a fair price, if you pick well. Support after delivery is the usual weak point, so agree it upfront.
Agency₹50,000 to ₹3,00,000+A team, a process, and metro overheads in the bill. Worth it for large portals and e-commerce, heavy for a local business.

What actually drives the price

Pages and features. A five page business site is simple work. Online payments, bookings, and customer logins are not. Language. A Telugu version doubles your reach in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, and few builders offer it. Content. Writing and photos done for you cost money and save you weeks. Support. A site nobody maintains slowly stops working.

What every business site needs, whatever it costs

It must open fast on a phone, show your number and WhatsApp clearly, appear on Google Maps, and bring you enquiries you can count. Anything less is a digital visiting card, not a business tool.
