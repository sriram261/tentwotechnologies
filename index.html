About TenTwo

We build technology for teams who are tired of almost finished software.

TenTwo Technologies is a software and engineering studio focused on shipping real products that solve real problems. We work with founders, operators, and growing teams who need software that holds up in production, not slides that look good in a pitch.

Our approach is simple. We listen, we plan, we build. Every line of code is written with the goal of making something measurable better for the business that uses it.

Founder note
Sriram Kumar Tentu
Founder and Director, TenTwo Technologies Private Limited

I started TenTwo Technologies because I was tired of watching good ideas get buried under bloated engineering. Companies kept shipping software that almost worked, almost shipped on time, almost moved the needle. I wanted to build a studio that did the opposite.

My background spans full stack development, cloud infrastructure, and AI systems across products that real people use every day. I have shipped web platforms, internal tools, and AI driven workflows for businesses operating in healthcare logistics, education, and SaaS.

Our promise. Every project gets engineered like it has to outlive the contract. That is the only way software is worth building.
